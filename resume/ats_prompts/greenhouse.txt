Greenhouse parses resumes with high accuracy. Follow these rules:
- Use standard section headers: Summary, Experience, Education, Skills, Projects
- No tables, no columns, no text boxes
- Bullet points should start with strong action verbs
- Include relevant keywords from the job description naturally in bullet points — do not keyword stuff
- Keep bullets concise: one line preferred, two lines max
- Quantify achievements wherever possible
- Skills section should be a clean comma-separated list, not a table
- Do not use special characters or icons
- Font and formatting do not matter in plain text — focus on content and keyword match
- Greenhouse's filter often looks for title matches — if the job title is "Software Engineer Intern" make sure that phrase or close variants appear naturally in the resume
