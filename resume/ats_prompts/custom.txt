Custom career sites are used by major tech companies (Amazon, Microsoft, Google, etc.) with proprietary ATS systems. Follow these rules:
- Use standard section headers: Summary, Experience, Education, Skills, Projects
- No tables, no columns, no text boxes, no special characters
- Bullet points must start with strong action verbs
- These companies receive massive application volumes — keyword matching is critical
- Include exact technologies, frameworks, and tools mentioned in the job description
- Quantify achievements with concrete metrics wherever possible
- Skills section should be a clean comma-separated list
- Keep bullets concise: one line preferred, two lines max
- Big tech values scale, impact, and technical depth — emphasize systems thinking and measurable results
- If the company has leadership principles or core values (e.g. Amazon's LPs), subtly align bullet language with them
- Include the exact job title or close variants in your summary
