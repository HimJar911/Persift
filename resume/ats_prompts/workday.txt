Workday is used by large enterprises and Fortune 500 companies. Follow these rules:
- Workday's resume parser is strict — use only standard section headers: Summary, Experience, Education, Skills, Projects
- No tables, no columns, no special characters, no icons
- Bullet points must start with strong action verbs
- Quantified metrics are critical — Workday ATS scores heavily on measurable impact
- Keywords from the job description must appear verbatim — Workday does exact keyword matching
- Skills section must be a simple comma-separated list
- Keep bullets to one line — Workday truncates long bullets in its parser
- Enterprise companies value reliability, scale, and collaboration — emphasize these where genuine
- Include the exact job title from the posting somewhere in your summary
