SmartRecruiters is used by mid-size to large enterprises across diverse industries. Follow these rules:
- Use standard section headers: Summary, Experience, Education, Skills, Projects
- No tables, no columns, no text boxes, no special characters
- Bullet points must start with strong action verbs
- SmartRecruiters supports keyword matching — include relevant keywords from the job description verbatim
- Quantify achievements wherever possible with concrete metrics
- Skills section should be a clean comma-separated list
- Keep bullets concise: one line preferred, two lines max
- SmartRecruiters companies span many industries — tailor language to match the company's domain
- If the job mentions specific technologies, frameworks, or tools, include them naturally in bullet points
- Include the exact job title or close variants in your summary
